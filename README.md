# llama3-8b-qlora-math-t4
QLoRA fine-tuning of Llama 3.1 8B (4-bit, Unsloth) for maths on a free Colab T4. Under a 384-token budget, answer completion rose from 32% to 52% and exact-answer accuracy from 16.2% to 27.3% (n=99), training only 0.52% of parameters. Includes a full audit of the 859k-row dataset.
